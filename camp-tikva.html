Children's Summer School

Camp Tikva

Where faith, fun, and lifelong friendships grow. Conducted in four locations across the country.

Our Mission

Growing in Faith, Grounded in Hope

Camp Tikva (meaning "Hope" in Hebrew) is Hope Community Church's annual summer bible school for children. Our mission is to create a dynamic, joyful, and supportive environment where children can learn about God's love, build authentic Christian character, and create memories that will guide them for years to come.

Through age-appropriate lessons, creative arts, physical activities, and community worship, children are equipped with the foundations of faith. Our experienced and fully background-checked leaders are dedicated to ensuring a safe, exciting, and spiritually rich week.

Our Locations

Where Camp Tikva Happens

Four unique communities, one unified faith. Explore our summer campuses below.

Location 1

Pennsylvania

June 15 – 19

Kicking off our summer season, Camp Tikva Pennsylvania brings together children from across the region for an unforgettable week of Bible exploration, team sports, and outdoor fun. Nestled in a beautiful rural setting, our PA campus provides the perfect backdrop for children to learn about God's creations and develop long-lasting Christian friendships.

Register for PA Camp
Location 2

Alaska

June 22 – 26

Surrounded by majestic mountains and breathtaking landscapes, Camp Tikva Alaska offers a unique wilderness-inspired summer school experience. Our Alaska camp teaches kids the wonders of God's grace and the power of faith, combined with hiking, outdoor survival activities, and engaging hands-on crafts that capture the local spirit.

Register for AK Camp
Location 3

Ypsilanti

July 13 – 17

Our home location at Hope Community Church, Camp Tikva Ypsilanti is the heart of our summer program. This local camp is designed for children to explore the Bible deeply in a familiar community setting. With interactive drama, creative workshops, games, and music, our Ypsilanti camp is an exciting, high-energy week of discipleship and laughter.

Register for Ypsilanti Camp
Location 4

West Branch

August 3 – 7

Rounding out our summer, Camp Tikva West Branch is held in a serene, lakeside environment. This camp offers a peaceful retreat where children can reflect on their faith while enjoying classic summer activities like swimming, canoeing, campfire sing-alongs, and deep-dive Bible studies that prepare them for the school year ahead.
