Anchor Bible College

A higher education institution dedicated to equipping Christians with rigorous, affordable biblical studies.

Deepen Your Calling

High-Quality, Flexible biblical Training

Anchor Bible College provides structured higher education solely designed for, directed toward, and attended by students preparing for Christian ministry.

Operating in Ypsilanti, Michigan, ABC merges rigorous academic study with active personal discipleship. Classes are taught by instructors with a pastoral calling, ensuring your theological education translates directly to spiritual and practical growth.

Whether you want to audit a single class for personal enrichment or build toward an accredited degree, ABC's hybrid structure fits cleanly into a busy schedule.

Yes. Anchor Bible College offers certificates and degrees in biblical studies but also offers the opportunity for students to take courses simply for their own personal edification and spiritual growth. Many students choose to audit classes or work at their own relaxed pace.
Anchor Bible College holds applicant status with the Association for Biblical Higher Education Commission on Accreditation (ABHE). ABHE is recognized by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation (CHEA) and the U.S. Department of Education, and is the premier accrediting body for biblical higher education.
Anchor Bible College is a semi-autonomous division of Hope Community Church. We utilize classroom facilities owned and operated by Hope Community Church, maintaining a strong operational relationship, while operating under our own independent board of directors in keeping with ABHE standards.
Thanks to generous donors, volunteer leadership, and shared facilities with Hope Community Church, we minimize institutional overhead. This allow us to pass direct savings to our students, keeping tuition at an affordable $75 per credit hour.
Yes. The office of post-secondary schools at the State of Michigan has authorized Anchor Bible College to operate as a religious higher education institution as a division of Hope Community Church, specifically designed to prepare students for Christian ministry.
