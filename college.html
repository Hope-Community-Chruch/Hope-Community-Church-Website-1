Anchor Bible College

A private Bible college in Ypsilanti, Michigan offering affordable, accessible biblical education and personal discipleship.

Why Anchor Bible College

Grow in Your Relationship with Christ

Anchor Bible College is a private Bible college in Ypsilanti, Michigan. It offers degrees in biblical studies and has as its mission to train Christians in the knowledge of God’s Word and in ministry for His kingdom.

Anchor Bible College provides an opportunity for high-quality biblical education along with interactive classes and personal discipleship from instructors with a pastoral calling and purpose.

Anchor Bible College is a semi-autonomous division of Hope Community Church and utilizes the facilities owned and operated by Hope Church.

Key Opportunities

What Makes ABC Different

🤝

Personal Interaction

In contrast to a world of increasing virtual life, Anchor Bible College offers an opportunity to learn and grow together with like-minded classmates and instructors.

🎓

High Quality Education

Highly qualified instructors, courses and programs in keeping with rigorous accreditation standards, and the technology and resources necessary for high quality education.

🌱

Spiritual Growth

Bible college studies aren’t just about factual knowledge. You will experience personal discipleship and an opportunity to accelerate your spiritual growth.

📜

Accreditation

Anchor Bible College holds applicant status with the Association for Biblical Higher Education (ABHE), recognized by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation and the U.S. Department of Education.

🔄

Transfer Credit Partners

Credits earned at ABC can be transferred through the National College Credit Recommendation Service (NCCRS) network of 1,300+ cooperating colleges and universities.

💰

Affordable Tuition

At just $75 per credit hour, ABC provides affordable tuition thanks to generous donations, volunteer staff, and its relationship with Hope Community Church.
