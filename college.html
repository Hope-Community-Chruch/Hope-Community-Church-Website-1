Anchor Bible College

Deepen your knowledge of God's Word — taught with passion and purpose.

Courses Offered

Build Your Biblical Foundation

Anchor Bible College offers structured biblical education for all levels. Whether you're new to faith or want to go deeper, our courses will equip and inspire you.

1
Introduction to the Bible
An overview of Scripture — from Genesis to Revelation. Perfect for new believers and seekers.
2
New Testament Studies
An in-depth exploration of the Gospels, Acts, and the letters of Paul and other apostles.
3
Old Testament Foundations
Understand the Law, the Prophets, and the Writings — and how they point to Christ.
4
Christian Living & Discipleship
Practical teaching on how to live faith out daily — relationships, finances, calling, and more.
5
Church History
2,000 years of the Christian story — persecution, reformation, revival, and the global church today.
6
Ministry & Leadership
For those called to serve — equipping leaders to shepherd well and lead with integrity.
