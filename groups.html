Discipleship Model

Meeting “From House to House”

The Bible says that the early Christians met together “from house to house” for teaching, fellowship and prayer. These were places where everyone could share and people quickly grew in their faith.

At Hope Church, we are committed to this pattern of meeting in small, home-sized groups. We call them Hope Groups. In fact, this is so important to us that you cannot become a member of Hope Church unless you are part of a Hope Group.

What happens at a Hope Group? Worship songs. Bible study. Sharing needs with one another. Counsel and care for one another. Prayer. Sometimes supper or a snack. Close relationships that extend throughout the week. Love.

👪 Should the children come?

Of course. This is God’s family meeting together. The children participate in most of the meeting and they have a great time.
