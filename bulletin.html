July 5, 2026 — Bulletin News

Weekly Bulletin

THE LORD’S SUPPER WILL BE SERVED during our worship today. All who know Jesus as Savior are invited to participate in taking communion.

THE PREACHING TODAY will be based on Daniel chapter 6, where we hear Daniel say, “My God sent His angel and shut the lions’ mouths.”

AFTER CHURCH TODAY, Camp Tikva volunteers will gather for an organizational meeting at 12:45 in the iFire room. They will receive assignments for next week’s Camp Tikva.

Are you ready for the Annual Church Picnic?

Here are the essential details:

  • The picnic is scheduled for this Saturday, July 11, at 1:00 pm.
  • The event will include a wonderful time of food, activities, worship, testimony, and Christian fellowship.
  • Please note that we can accommodate any kind of weather. Please come, rain or shine.
  • Lily Peterson is arranging food for the picnic. You may sign up by calling Lily at 734-368-7358.

LINA SOPER INVITES YOU to participate in the Picnic Freebie Exchange. Do you have clothing or household items you would like to give away? Are there things you need that you would like to receive? A display area will be set up and monitored from 1:00-4:00 during the church picnic.

NEXT SUNDAY, after church, there will be a mandatory meeting for Camp Tikva volunteers. This will ensure safety for the children, as well as spiritual preparation for those who serve.

CAMP TIKVA IS ONE WEEK AWAY! The program will begin at 10:00 am on Monday morning, July 13. It will end each day at 3:30 pm. This year we will study the book of Genesis.

ALSO COMING IN JULY, our church will send a ministry team to Shawanaga, Ontario from July 18 to 25.

IT IS TIME TO REGISTER FOR A CAMP SITE for the annual Hope Church Camping trip. The annual camping trip will be August 14-16. To secure a camp site, please contact our Camping Captain, Ben Ferguson at 734-223-2404.
