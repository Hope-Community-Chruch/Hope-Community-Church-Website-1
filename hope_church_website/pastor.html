Meet the Pastor

A heart for God, a love for people, and a passion for the Word.

Senior Pastor

Pastor Manwiller

Pastor Manwiller has dedicated his life to guiding the Hope Community Church family with warmth, wisdom, and unwavering faith. His messages are practical, scripture-based, and deeply encouraging.

"My heart is to see every person encounter the living God — not just to know about Him, but to truly know Him."

He and his family have been rooted in the Ypsilanti community for decades, building friendships across every walk of life. Whether you're new to faith or a lifelong believer, you'll feel right at home under his leadership.

📖 Bible-Led

Every message grounded in Scripture.

🙏 Prayer-Centered

A church built on conversation with God.

🤝 Community-Driven

Faith lived out together, not in isolation.

❤️ Grace-Filled

A place for every person, exactly as they are.
